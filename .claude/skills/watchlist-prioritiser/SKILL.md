## Flagging Rules

- **Always surface** any message, email, Teams post, or WhatsApp from a watchlist
  contact — even if no reply is needed yet
- **Flag voice notes** from watchlist contacts that Kelly hasn't listened to
- **Flag reply overdue** if Kelly hasn't responded to a watchlist contact in
  more than 3 days
- **Prioritise by project urgency:** TSPC and Discovery Life are active billable
  delivery — flag their contacts first; Old Mutual and Quro are pre-sales /
  pipeline — flag but lower prominence unless time-sensitive
- **Suveer Ramdhani** messages always surface regardless of topic — internal
  alignment is business-critical

---

## Channel-Specific Behaviour

### Email
- Tag watchlist items under the **WATCHLIST** section in the brief
- Include: sender, one-line topic, inbox name
- Do not require a reply action to surface — presence alone is enough

### WhatsApp
- Flag direct messages from watchlist contacts
- Flag group messages only if the watchlist contact directly addresses Kelly
- Always flag unlistened voice notes from watchlist contacts

### Microsoft Teams
- Flag direct messages and @mentions from watchlist contacts
- Flag if a watchlist contact posts in a channel Kelly follows, even without
  a direct mention

---

## Exclusions

- Do not apply watchlist logic to automated notifications, bot posts, or
  calendar invites — these follow the FYI/count-only rule
- Do not surface commercial/procurement back-and-forth from watchlist contacts
  unless they directly ask Kelly to act, decide, or respond
